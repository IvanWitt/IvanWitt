# IvanWitt - Security & Privacy Policy

## Security

### Data Collection
IvanWitt **does NOT collect any personal data**. All operations are local to your device.

### Network Requests
- Requests are only made to video platforms (YouTube, BiliBili, etc.)
- Login credentials are stored locally and never sent to IvanWitt servers
- No telemetry or analytics tracking

### Security Best Practices
- Keep your Android OS updated
- Use strong authentication for linked accounts
- Review app permissions in your settings
- Don't share your app backups with untrusted parties

## Privacy

### What We Don't Do
- ❌ Track your watch history
- ❌ Sell your data
- ❌ Display ads or sponsored content
- ❌ Require registration
- ❌ Send data to third parties

### What IvanWitt Stores Locally
- Your subscriptions
- Watch history (if enabled)
- Playlists
- Cookies (for login purposes)
- Preferences & settings

### Permissions

#### Required
- **INTERNET** - To fetch video content
- **WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE** - For downloads

#### Optional
- **CAMERA** - For QR code scanning
- **MICROPHONE** - For voice search
- **LOCATION** - For geolocation-based content

You can deny optional permissions in your device settings.

## Third-Party Services

IvanWitt integrates with optional services:

### SponsorBlock
- Open-source crowdsourced sponsorship database
- Privacy policy: https://sponsor.ajay.app
- Can be disabled in settings

### ReturnYouTubeDislike
- Community-powered dislike count restoration
- Privacy policy: https://returnyoutubedislike.com
- Can be disabled in settings

## Data Deletion

To delete all IvanWitt data:
1. Settings → Apps → IvanWitt
2. Storage → Clear Storage
3. Or uninstall the app

## Report Security Issues

If you find a security vulnerability:
- **DO NOT** open a public issue
- Email security details to the maintainers
- Include steps to reproduce and potential impact

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected in this document with a new date.
